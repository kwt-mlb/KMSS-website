About KMSS

Why we exist

Moving 12,000 kilometres to study is not just an academic decision — it is a logistical, financial and emotional one. KMSS exists so that no Kuwaiti student in Melbourne has to work all of that out alone.

We've been running since 2023. Since then: Kuwaiti bazaars, Eid celebrations, snow trips to Mount Buller, padel tournaments, workshops — and a guide to this city written by students who already made every mistake for you.

Every committee member is a volunteer and a full-time student. Everything we run is funded by ticket sales at cost and by sponsors who genuinely serve students.
